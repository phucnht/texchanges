## A review protocol that stays in your source

Texchanges gives authors, reviewers, and editing tools a shared vocabulary for additions, removals, replacements, highlights, comments, and decisions. Stable IDs make individual changes addressable without introducing a proprietary document format.

```latex
\txdefineauthor[name={Phuc Nguyen},color=orange]{phuc}
\txreplace[author=phuc,id=R12,status=pending]{old text}{new text}
```

Use `review` while decisions remain open. Switch to `final` for the proposed document or `original` to recover its initial state.
